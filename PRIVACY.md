# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Snackdraft is provided by Sergii Ziborov. This policy describes the current beta for iPhone and iPad.

## Data stored on your device

Snackdraft stores level stars, discovered ingredients and recipes, sound and haptic preferences, and whether you have seen the playing guide. These are stored in the app's local Application Support folder and preferences. Your device's backup settings may include this data in an Apple-managed backup.

The game has no account system or developer-operated game server. We do not receive your tray, progress, or preferences.

## Advertising and tracking

This beta contains no advertising SDK, advertisements, developer analytics, or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Advertising is planned for a future release. Before a release enables it, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will identify the advertising provider, its data use, and the available privacy choices.

## App Store purchases

A one-time Remove Ads upgrade is planned. It is not offered for sale in this beta. If purchases become available, Apple will process payments using your Apple Account. The game will use verified App Store transaction information to recognize the upgrade and restore it. We will not receive your card number or payment credentials.

## Apple services and TestFlight

Apple operates the App Store, device backups, and TestFlight under its own terms and privacy policies. TestFlight collects beta crash logs and usage information and makes beta testing information available to the developer. If you submit feedback, it can include your comments, screenshots, and contact information. We use that information to investigate issues and improve the game. Do not include sensitive information in feedback.

Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
TestFlight terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/

## Sharing and contacting support

When you choose Share, Snackdraft creates a tray image on the device and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination; the receiving app or service has its own privacy policy. Snackdraft does not upload the image to a developer server.

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. We retain support correspondence only while it is needed to resolve your issue or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

## Your choices

Reset progress in Settings deletes the current locally saved stars and discoveries. It does not change Apple backups, TestFlight reports, or any future App Store purchase entitlement. Deleting the app removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore that data. You can manage backups through Apple's settings.

## Children

Snackdraft does not ask players to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it. Any future advertising release will document its audience and privacy controls before advertisements are enabled.

## Updates and contact

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change and revise the effective date. The policy is available inside Settings.
