# About Snackdraft

Pick one bite. Build a delicious little world.

Snackdraft is a cozy, single-player puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Choose one of three ingredients each turn, then place it on a 4×4 tasting tray. Connect ingredients by their sides to make recipes, discover combinations, and travel through nine kitchens.

## A kitchen for every mood

Journey has 70 levels, 33 ingredients, 71 recipes, and 10 achievements. Free Play lets you cook at your own pace in an unlocked kitchen. Today's tray offers a date-seeded challenge.

## Made for little breaks

There is no account to create. Your stars, discoveries, and preferences are saved on this device. Sound cues, kitchen ambience, and haptics can all be adjusted in Settings.

## Free to play

All gameplay is free. Advertising and an optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase are planned for the release. This beta does not display advertisements or sell an ad-removal upgrade. When available, the App Store will show the localized price before you confirm a purchase.
