# Help & Support

## How to play

Pick one of the three offered ingredients, then tap or drag it into an empty tray cell. Connect a recipe's ingredients by their sides. An L shape works; diagonal-only contact does not. One tray can make several recipes, even when they share ingredients.

Serve when a recipe is ready, or keep building for more combinations. Filling all 16 cells is optional. Tap the question-mark button during a game to replay the illustrated guide.

## Progress and settings

Your stars and discoveries are saved on this device. Settings lets you adjust haptics, sound cues, and kitchen music. Reset progress deletes game progress after confirmation. Deleting the app can remove local progress.

## Purchases

## Report an issue

Email sergii.ziborov@gmail.com with the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot of the game can help. Do not include passwords, card details, or other sensitive information.

If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from TestFlight or the screenshot feedback option.
